## Thesis

At the University of Bonn I supervised Bachelor's and Master's theses in Geography within the SeBAS (Sensing Biodiversity Across Scales) project, mostly on remote sensing of grassland and forest biodiversity with machine learning. I have also acted as first and second examiner for Bachelor's theses at the Geographical Institute.

### Master's theses

<ul class="thesis-list">
  <li><strong>Jonas Spekker</strong> (2023) – Spatio-temporal analysis of biodiversity using remote sensing and machine learning in managed grasslands in Germany.</li>
  <li><strong>Robin Cremer</strong> (2022) – Application of machine learning and remote sensing to predict spatial variability of maize yields in the context of conservation agriculture in Southern Africa.</li>
  <li><strong>Daniel Blum</strong> (2021) – The potential of Sentinel-1 radar images for inferring vegetation structure in grasslands: a case study of biodiversity exploratories in Germany.</li>
</ul>

### Bachelor's theses

<ul class="thesis-list">
  <li><strong>Jonas Pischke</strong> (2022) – Leaf area index retrieval in grasslands in Germany using random forest regression and multitemporal UAV and Sentinel-2 imagery.</li>
  <li><strong>Benjamin Buchendorfer</strong> (2022) – Mapping functional diversity and biomass production of grassland in Germany using UAV images and random forest modelling.</li>
  <li><strong>Janik Hoffmann</strong> (2022) – Predicting tree species and structural diversity across temperate forest types with satellite remote sensing and deep learning.</li>
  <li><strong>Leonie Hartog</strong> (2022) – Leaf area index and biomass estimation with PlanetScope imagery and machine learning of grasslands in Germany.</li>
  <li><strong>Julian Bott</strong> (2021) – Assessing the possibilities of Sentinel-1 time series and the Omnibus algorithm to detect grassland management practices: a case study in the Swabian Jura.</li>
  <li><strong>Johannes Wilk</strong> (2021) – Spatiotemporal analysis of drought impacts on vegetation using high and moderate resolution satellite time series.</li>
  <li><strong>Moritz Rubach</strong> (2021) – Investigation of the relationship between remote sensing data from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 and land use in temperate grassland areas in Germany.</li>
  <li><strong>Maximilian Roth</strong> (2020) – Deriving an essential biodiversity variable on ecosystem fragmentation with remote sensing in German grasslands.</li>
</ul>
