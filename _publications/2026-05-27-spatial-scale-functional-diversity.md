excerpt: 'We examined the magnitude of plant functional diversity across the plot, farm and regional scale in three temperate regions of Central Europe, and the impact of land-use intensity at the plot and farm scale as well as on farm-to-regional functional dissimilarity. Functional richness increased from the plot to the farm scale and evenness showed a unimodal trend, while land-use effects were weak but similar across scales.'
