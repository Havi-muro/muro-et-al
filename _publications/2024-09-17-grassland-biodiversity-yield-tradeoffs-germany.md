excerpt: 'We estimated the causal effect of mowing frequency on plant species richness for all 1.3 million permanent grassland fields of Germany over four years, using remote sensing-based proxies of management and biodiversity. More frequent mowing reduced species richness on almost every field, but the size of the loss varied with climate, field structure and farm context, and spatially targeted extensification cut the cost of an additional species from 126 to 51 euros.'
