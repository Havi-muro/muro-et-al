excerpt: 'Going beyond the plot scale, we investigate the field and farm scale in two German regions, using prediction maps of species richness, biomass and land-use intensity upscaled from Sentinel-2 imagery. High land-use intensity directly reduced species richness and its spatial variability, both of which mediated rather than buffered the spatial variability of biomass production, and the biodiversity-ecosystem functioning relationship slightly strengthened with spatial scale.'
