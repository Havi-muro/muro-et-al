excerpt: 'We developed a remote sensing-based framework that identifies priority locations for connectivity conservation from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 imagery, and applied it to African elephants in the Kilombero catchment of the SAGCOT in Tanzania. The land cover and habitat map reached an overall accuracy of 75%, the connectivity model found 214 potential corridors, and 43 of them were prioritised for protection and 43 for restoration.'
